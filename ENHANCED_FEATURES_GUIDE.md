## ?? How It Works - User Journey

### Discovery Flow
```
1. USER OPENS APP
   ?
2. SEES CHECK-INS (People at locations)
3. SEES ACTIVITIES (Events happening nearby)
   ?
4. FILTERS BY INTERESTS
   - Sports? Yoga? Gaming? Art?
   - Amateur or professional?
   - Free or willing to pay?
   ?
5. JOINS ACTIVITY OR CHECKS IN
   ?
6. MEETS LIKE-MINDED PEOPLE
   ?
7. RATES ACTIVITY/PEOPLE
   ?
8. BUILDS CONNECTIONS
```
