? MEENGLE - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

?? Getting Started

How do I create a MEENGLE account? ?

1. Download the MEENGLE app or visit meengle.com
2. Click "Sign Up"
3. Enter your email and create a password
4. Verify your email
5. Complete your profile (add photos, bio, interests)
6. Start discovering matches!

Do I need to use my real name and photo? ?

Yes. MEENGLE requires authentic profiles to maintain trust and safety. We ask for your real first name and clear, recent photos of yourself. Catfishing is against our community guidelines and may result in account suspension.

Is MEENGLE safe? ?

MEENGLE has multiple safety features: photo verification, profile review, blocking/reporting tools, and AI fraud detection. However, no app is 100% safe. Always meet in public, verify information independently, and trust your instincts. Never send money to someone you haven't met.

What age do I need to be to use MEENGLE? ?

You must be at least 18 years old. We verify age during signup and remove underage accounts immediately.

?? Matching & Messaging

How does the matching algorithm work? ?

MEENGLE uses AI-powered matching based on: location, age preferences, interests, values, activity, and mutual interests. The algorithm learns from your interactions to improve suggestions over time.

Can I search for specific people? ?

Yes! Use Advanced Search to filter by age, location, interests, height, education, and more. You can also adjust your preferences in Settings to refine what MEENGLE shows you.

What happens if I like someone? ?

If you like someone and they like you back, it's a Match! You can then message each other. If they haven't seen your profile yet, they'll see a notification that you liked them.

Can I unsend a message? ?

You can delete messages you send within 1 hour. The recipient will see "[Message deleted]" but your conversation history remains. You cannot permanently delete an entire chat without blocking.

Can I block someone? ?

Yes. Click the ? menu on their profile or message and select "Block." They cannot see your profile, message you, or match with you again. You can unblock anytime in your blocked list.

?? Privacy & Security

Will my friends see my MEENGLE profile? ?

MEENGLE doesn't connect to your social media. Your profile is only visible to other MEENGLE users. You can block specific people if you'd prefer they don't see your profile.

Does MEENGLE sell my data? ?

No. We do NOT sell personal data to advertisers, brokers, or third parties. We may share data with service providers (Stripe, SendGrid, AWS) who help run MEENGLE under strict contracts.

Can I export my data? ?

Yes. Go to Settings ? Data & Privacy ? Download My Data. You'll receive a copy of your profile info, messages, photos, and activity in JSON format within 7 days.

How do I delete my account? ?

Go to Settings ? Account ? Delete Account. Confirm your password and decision. Your account will be deleted within 30 days. Data like messages and photos are deleted within 90 days.

?? Support & Issues

How do I report someone for inappropriate behavior? ?

Click the ? menu on their profile or message and select "Report." Choose the reason (harassment, spam, inappropriate content, etc.) and provide details. Our team reviews all reports within 24 hours.

My account was suspended. Why? ?

Accounts may be suspended for violating Community Guidelines: fake profiles, harassment, explicit content, fraud, hate speech, etc. If you believe it's a mistake, email support@meengle.com with details.

I forgot my password. How do I reset it? ?

On the login page, click "Forgot Password." Enter your email, and we'll send a reset link. Click the link and create a new password. The link expires in 24 hours.

I'm experiencing technical issues. What should I do? ?

1. Try refreshing the page/app
2. Clear your browser cache and cookies
3. Update to the latest app version
4. Restart your device
If issues persist, contact support@meengle.com with details and screenshots.
